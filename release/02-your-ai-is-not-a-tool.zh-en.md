现在到处都能听到一句话:"AI 只是个工具,关键看你怎么用。"

You hear it everywhere now: "AI is just a tool. What matters is what we do with it."

Sacasas 不同意。他的说法是:**AI 不是工具,是一个你身处其中的环境。**

Sacasas disagrees. His claim: **your AI is not a tool. It is an environment, and you are in it.**

区别在哪?工具是你能拿起、也能放下的东西。锤子用完放回抽屉,它不会在你睡觉的时候改变你。环境不一样——你只要待在里面,它就在改变你,不管你用得多认真。

What's the difference? A tool is something you can pick up and put down. You return the hammer to the drawer and it does not reshape you while you sleep. An environment is not like that: as long as you are inside it, it works on you, however carefully you use it.

## "只要用得好就没事",这话我们已经试过一次了

## "It'll be fine as long as we use it well" — we already ran this experiment

智能手机和社交媒体刚出来时,人们也说它们"只是工具,看你怎么用"。

When smartphones and social media arrived, they too were called "just tools — it's how you use them."

二十年下来,结论很清楚:哪怕是意志力最强、最克制的人,刷久了注意力也变碎、脾气也变急。不是因为他们"用错了",是因为这东西天天在手上,你躲不开它的影响。

After nearly twenty years the result is plain: a so-called tool like a smartphone or a social media platform will re-form even the most vigilant and disciplined user into its own image and shape — attention fragmented, patience shortened. Not because they used it wrong, but because it was in their hands every day and its effects could not be evaded.

现在轮到 AI,又有人说"学会用好就行"。Sacasas 觉得这话轻飘飘的。

Now the same is said of AI: just learn to use it well. Sacasas is astounded at how blithely this is asserted.

He offers an example. A reader inside a very large company — 600,000 people — says he gets to watch this run in fast-forward:

公司里对 AI 最谨慎的那些团队,慢慢开始只选"稳妥"的方案,而不是"更好"的方案。该冒的险不冒了,该拍板的不拍了,凡事先看 AI 会怎么说、别人一般怎么做。

The teams using AI most carefully are the ones losing the ability to tell a good option from a merely safe one. The risk worth taking is not taken; the call that should be made is not made; every question first runs through "what would the AI say, what do others usually do."

这位读者的原话是:"这种改变不会放过认真的人,它专挑认真的人下手。"

The reader's own words: "The malformation doesn't skip the diligent. It recruits them."

Sacasas 一个做计算机的朋友也这么说:他能感觉到自己的判断力在悄悄下滑,也看得出来年轻同事退得更快。

A computer scientist Sacasas had lunch with said much the same: he could feel the subtle shifts in his own awareness and judgment, and could plainly see the sharper effects on junior colleagues.

## 为什么"小心用"救不了你

## Why "using it carefully" doesn't save you

关键在这里:**改变你的,不是 AI 给了你哪个具体答案,而是你开始事事都过一遍 AI 这件事本身。**

Here is the point: **what changes you is not which particular answer the AI hands you, but the fact that you now route everything through it.**

比如你让 AI 帮你写个东西,它给了五版建议,你四版都否了。看起来你很有主见。但你其实已经在"改它的稿",而不是"从头自己想"了。最后成品往往比你自己写的更平庸——而每一个字都是你亲手定的,你全程感觉是自己写出来的。

Say you ask AI to help you write something. It offers five versions; you reject four. You look decisive. But you are already editing its draft rather than thinking from scratch. The finished piece is usually more generic than what you would have written — and every word was your own choice, and it felt the whole time like you wrote it.

评论者查理·约翰逊说得准:**你可以拒绝 AI 的每一条建议,最后还是会走到一个你自己到不了的地方,而且一路上都觉得是自己走过去的。**

The critic Charley Johnson put it exactly: **you can turn down every suggestion and still end up somewhere you wouldn't have reached alone, feeling the whole time like you walked there on your own two feet.**

工具你能放下,环境会把你圈在里面。

You can put a tool down; the environment absorbs you into itself.

## 半个世纪前,有人就说破了

## Someone named this half a century ago

麦克卢汉——上世纪研究"新技术怎么改变人"的学者——几十年前就讲过:一天到晚争论"内容好不好、用得对不对",是没看懂门道。他打了个比方:一样新东西(电视、手机、现在的 AI)端到你面前时,你盯着看的那些"内容",就像**小偷进门时随身带的一块肉,专门丢给看门狗、让它别叫**。你盯着那块肉,真正进屋的东西反而没看见。

Marshall McLuhan — who spent the last century studying how new technologies change us — said decades ago that the standard response, "it's how you use it that counts," is the numb stance of the technological idiot. His image: the "content" that a new thing (television, the phone, now AI) puts in front of you is **like the juicy piece of meat carried by the burglar to distract the watchdog of the mind**. You watch the meat; you miss what actually enters the house.

那个"真正进屋的东西",就是它悄悄改掉你**看世界的方式**——不动声色,你还来不及反应。

What actually enters is a change in **how you perceive** — altering your patterns of perception steadily and without any resistance.

## "一件需要谨慎使用的宝贵工具"——没说到点上

## "A valuable tool that requires vigilance" — this misses the mark

有人试着承认 AI"不中立",说它"带着开发者和使用者的影子"。Sacasas 的朋友巴尔巴-凯说,这其实还是"中立论"的老调:能干好事也能干坏事,看你选。绕一圈,结论还是"AI 是好东西,小心用就行"。

Some try to grant that AI is "not neutral," saying it "takes on the characteristics of those who devise, finance, regulate and use it." Sacasas's friend Antón Barba-Kay notes this is precisely what people mean when they call a technology neutral: it can be used for good or ill, and it's up to us to choose well. The loop closes on the same conclusion — "a valuable tool that requires vigilance."

巴尔巴-凯回了一句狠的:这话在他听来,等于说"可卡因也是种好药,吸的时候记得就着点盐"。

Barba-Kay's retort: to some of us that is interchangeable with saying cocaine can be a valuable drug that should be snorted with a pinch of salt.

他的意思是:叫"工具",就默认它有固定几种用途;可 AI 像语言一样,什么事都能渗进去。**它和毒品一样,改的不是某件具体的事,是你做判断、做选择时的整个状态。**

His point: the language of "tools" implies a set of circumscribed, targeted uses, whereas AI is as multifarious as language. **Like drugs, it transforms the very conditions of our choosing and thinking.**

## 那到底该怎么办

## So what do we do

Sacasas 的答案不是"多懂点技术、学会分辨信息真假"——那还是停在"想明白就行"的层面。

Sacasas's answer is not media literacy in a cognitivist mode — that still sits at the level of "just think it through."

他说:我们早就从"工具时代"进了"系统时代"。工具你能站在它外面看,系统你没法站在外面,因为你就在里面。

We passed, he says, from the age of tools to the age of systems. You can stand over a tool; you cannot stand outside a system, because you are within it.

他借哲学家伊里奇的话开了个方子:需要一种"新的自律"——有意识地把自己的感官夺回来,重新相信自己亲眼看到、亲手摸到的东西,而不是屏幕告诉你的。

Borrowing from Ivan Illich, he prescribes a new asceticism: a disciplined reclaiming of the senses, a promptitude to obey your own experience rather than what the screen reports.

伊里奇还讲过一句让 Sacasas 一直记着的话:这种自律,最后连着的是**友谊**。这些数字环境损害的不只是我们的脑子,还有人和人之间的关系。

Illich drew a line that has always struck Sacasas: this discipline of perception runs all the way to **friendship**. These digital environments are not only an epistemic threat but a threat to the social fabric.

**所以至少先把这个念头放下:AI 只是一件你需要学会好好使用的工具。**

**So, at the very least, dispense with the idea that AI is just a tool we need to learn to use wisely.**
